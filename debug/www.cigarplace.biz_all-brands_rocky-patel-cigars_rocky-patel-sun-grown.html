Rocky Patel Sun Grown

Rocky Patel Sun Grown was one of Rocky's first big hits and it still remains a classic and popular cigar to this day. Made in sunny Honduras, this fantastic smoke contains a complex multi-country blend of filler tobaccos from Nicaragua, and the Dominican Republic. This mixture, aged for more than 7 years, is bound with a stout Nicaraguan leaf and topped off with a rich Ecuadorian sun grown wrapper. The wrapper itself has 5 years of aging on it, making this very complex and memorable cigar a must-have in any serious humidor.


The Rocky Patel Sun Grown Series has earned multiple 90+ ratings from Cigar Aficionado, praised for its rich, balanced flavors and exceptional craftsmanship. Starting off with a rich, earthy base note, Sun Grown transforms throughout the experience into a medley of woody and tobacco flavors. There are notes of savory spice and white pepper and a subtle, sweet finish. This cigar was once considered full-bodied, but compared to the monsters of today it's a very enjoyable medium-bodied smoke.


Rocky Patel Sun Grown remains one of the top sellers from Rocky Patel. It's a great entry into the world of fine, complex smokes for any beginner looking to graduate to a more sophisticated type of stick. This cigar pairs well with bourbon and makes a wonderful after-dinner treat. Comparable cigars include Rocky Patel 15th Anniversary and Pinar Del Rio. Taste the early success of Rocky Patel with a Sun Grown. You won't be disappointed.(Shop all Rocky Patel Cigars)
